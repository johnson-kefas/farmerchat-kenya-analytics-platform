# Kenya boundary source

The bundled county and national GeoJSON files are the simplified Kenya ADM1
and ADM0 layers from the geoBoundaries `gbOpen` collection.

- County boundary ID: `KEN-ADM1-32016919`
- National boundary ID: `KEN-ADM0-21065076`
- Boundary year represented: 2020
- County units: 47
- Source organizations: RCMRD GeoPortal, Africa GeoPortal, and geoBoundaries
- Licence reported by the geoBoundaries API: Public Domain
- Retrieved: 9 August 2026

Official metadata:

- <https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/KEN/ADM1/>
- <https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/KEN/ADM0/>

The user-supplied `Admo.zip` archive was inspected but not redistributed. It
contained one national feature and 47 county features in WGS 84, but it did not
include source or licence documentation. The openly licensed geoBoundaries
layers were therefore selected for the deployment package.
